# TRL 30-Day Execution Pack — Weeks 1 & 2 (Foundation + Momentum)

**The complete 30-day launch system for TRL Service clients.**

This pack completes the full arc: Weeks 1–4.

## 📅 Week 1 — Foundation & Setup (Days 1–7)

### Day 1: Onboarding & Asset Collection
- Welcome call / message (15 min)
- Collect: logo, brand colors, current website / IG / LinkedIn
- Define 3 core services / offers to promote
- Confirm WhatsApp Business + payment method

### Day 2: Identity & Positioning
- Finalize 30-second identity kit:
  - Instagram bio (150 chars)
  - LinkedIn headline
  - WhatsApp status / about
- Write 3-sentence positioning statement

### Day 3–4: Content Foundation
- Create 5 high-quality posts (mix of value + proof)
  1. Problem agitation post
  2. "How we do it differently" post
  3. Client result / case study teaser
  4. Behind-the-scenes / process
  5. Strong CTA post

### Day 5: Automation Blueprint
- Map 2–3 key automations needed for the client
- Define lead sources + follow-up sequences
- Draft WhatsApp / email templates

### Day 6–7: Tech Setup
- Set up basic CRM / Google Sheet tracker
- Connect first simple automation (lead capture → WhatsApp)
- Test end-to-end flow

---

## 📅 Week 2 — Content Engine + First Leads (Days 8–14)

### Day 8–9: Content Calendar
- Build 14-day content calendar (10 posts total for first 2 weeks)
- Schedule 5 posts
- Create 3 story templates + 2 reels ideas

### Day 10: Lead Magnet
- Create one simple lead magnet (PDF checklist, mini-audit, or template)
- Set up landing page or link-in-bio

### Day 11–12: Outreach Sprint
- Run 30 targeted DMs / comments per day
- Use the 3-post sequence for warm outreach
- Log all conversations in tracker

### Day 13: First Automation Live
- Activate the first full workflow (e.g. lead → nurture → booking)
- Monitor for 24 hours

### Day 14: Review & Adjust
- Weekly review meeting
- Analyze what converted
- Adjust messaging + double down on winners

---

## 🎯 Deliverables by End of Week 2

- ✅ 10 posts created + scheduled
- ✅ 30-second identity kit (IG + LinkedIn + WA)
- ✅ 2–3 working automations
- ✅ Lead tracking system
- ✅ First 5–15 qualified leads in pipeline
- ✅ Content calendar for next 14 days
